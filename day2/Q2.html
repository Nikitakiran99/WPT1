In HTML, the font you choose will play a major role in the look and feel of your web pages. You get to pick the font's color, weight, size, and so on. And all these features make your websites and apps look better and more presentable to the user. With the font-size property in CSS, you can change how big or small the text is on the web page. You can use this property in any type of CSS you are writing – external, internal, or inline. Inline CSS is one of the three different ways you can use to style any HTML element.
